## Architectural root cause
Acceptance validated a capability answer by RE-PARSING its prose (`analyzeCapabilityProse` -> scope/polarity claims). The runtime envelope had no authoritative structured truth (`factPayload: null`), so every improvement to the parser (clause segmentation, claim objects, composition, time, modality) could still be out-phrased by the next realistic construction (named external products, retraction, idioms, negated quantifiers, scope composition). A parser of natural language cannot be made the authority over natural language.

## Contract (`supabase/functions/_shared/productTruthContract.ts`) - authority flow: canonical authority -> structured truth -> prose
`factPayload` (discriminated on `kind: 'product_truth'`, additive, no contract-version bump) = `{ kind, schemaVersion:1, capabilityId, truthStatus, claimScope, source, marketScope, currencyScope, registryState, minimumPlan, requiredRole, accountPlan, accountRole, accountMarket, accountEntitlement, comparedCapabilityIds }`, all fields always present, canonical key order.
- `truthStatus`: AVAILABLE | NOT_AVAILABLE | PLAN_LOCKED | ROLE_LOCKED | MARKET_UNAVAILABLE | PAYMENT_NOT_LIVE | INVOICING_NOT_ISSUED | COMPARISON | ACCOUNT_MARKET | CLARIFICATION | UNKNOWN (ACCOUNT_MARKET is an addition to the brief's list, needed for the account currency statement).
- `claimScope`: PRODUCT | ACCOUNT | MARKET | CAPABILITY | NOT_APPLICABLE. `currencyScope`: ILS|USD|EUR|GBP|ACCOUNT|MULTI|NOT_APPLICABLE|UNKNOWN. `source`: CAPABILITY_REGISTRY | BILLING_FACTS | INVOICING_FACTS | MARKET_RULES | COMPARISON_RULES | CLARIFICATION_RULES | ACCOUNT_LIFECYCLE_RULES.
- Self-consistency validator (`validateProductTruthPayload`): a concrete currency is only ever ACCOUNT- or MARKET-scoped and only for its own market (ILS <-> LOCAL; USD/EUR/GBP/MULTI <-> INTERNATIONAL) - "TEKANGO prices are ILS only" as a PRODUCT-wide fact is not expressible. Gate contradictions (PLAN_LOCKED without a plan, AVAILABLE with a denied entitlement, ...) are invalid.
- `accountPlan` is the EFFECTIVE tier under the canonical account-entitlement rule (a super_admin is entitled at the top tier).
