## Root causes
**Blocker 1.** `thirdPartyPerson` was a BLACKLIST of contexts: a person noun counted as a third party only after a possessive, a determiner (the / this ...) or a preposition (for / to / of ...). A person noun directly modifying a display noun ("customer prices", "client totals", "user fees", "supplier costs"), a possessive "customer's prices" (the token `customer's` was tokenized as an UNKNOWN word, so no person noun was ever seen) and the Hebrew construct forms ("מחיר לקוח", "מחירי לקוח", "תמחור לקוח") matched none of the contexts, passed the guard and then satisfied the display-object rule, entering ACCOUNT_MARKET. Measured on the generated sets the v37 classifier over-routed 5,760 / 8,064 English and 576 / 1,440 Hebrew cases (all 6 Codex negatives).
**Blocker 2.** Normalization split a Hebrew prefix from what follows only when a hyphen preceded a LATIN LETTER (`ב-TEKANGO`). `ב-£`, `ב-$`, `ב-€`, `ב-₪` (and an attached `ב£`) stayed one unknown token, so the currency never reached the tokenizer: 168 / 168 symbol cases failed on v37.

## Changes (`supabase/functions/chat-ai/marketIntentGrammar.ts` only; `marketTruth.ts`, `index.ts`, payload schema, renderer unchanged)
1. **Inverted the guard into POSITIVE PROOF** (`selfProvenPerson` + `thirdPartyPerson`): a person noun (customer, client, user, supplier, vendor, employee, member, contact ...) in a currency clause is allowed ONLY when it is the PREDICATE of a SELF identity - a SELF subject reached by walking back over fillers (adverbs, determiners, market words, negation, modals) and, in English, the copula / participle ("we are (really) international customers", "אנחנו (בעצם) לקוחות בינלאומיים", "are we overseas clients?"). Everywhere else - bare compounds, possessives, "my clients", "the client", "for the customer", Hebrew construct / definite / prefixed forms - the person noun names a third party and blocks the currency intent. One structural rule replaces the context list; no phrase list. The same guard covers the "change my currency / market" rule.
2. **Possessive person nouns**: `customer's` is tokenized as the PERSON noun (genitive flag) instead of an unknown word.
3. **Hebrew noun / verb ambiguity**: "משתמש" (user / uses) and "עובד" (employee / works) keep BOTH readings in the lexicon (the verb reading is added, never replaces the person entry - it had silently overwritten "עובד"); right after an account / possessive / brand subject the word is the VERB ("איזה מטבע החשבון שלי משתמש?", "TEKANGO עובד"), so the stricter guard causes no regression.
4. **Normalization**: a Hebrew prefix + hyphen + (Latin letter OR currency symbol `$ € £ ₪`) is split, and a prefix attached straight to a symbol is split (`ב-£` -> `ב £`); the split prefix is a preposition and the symbol a canonical currency. Digits after the symbol still hit the number guard (a price is not a preference).
5. Lexicon: Hebrew construct forms "מחירי", "תמחורי"; "בא לי" (I feel like) now normalizes to subject SELF.
